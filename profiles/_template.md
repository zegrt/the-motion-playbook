# Profile: <name>

A profile has two halves, because the person directing and the person at the controls can be different people.

- **Taste** follows whoever **directs** the project. On work they direct, their taste overrides the core and the house
  defaults where the two differ.
- **Working style** follows whoever **runs the session** (the person at the tool, or working with an AI agent). It sets
  how much the agent does on its own, how often to show work, and how to write.

Order of authority: the project's own decisions, then the director's taste, then the house layer, then the core.
Start small: a few lines you're sure of are enough, and an empty item is fine. The profile grows from the corrections
you find yourself repeating: when you correct the same thing twice, the agent proposes a line for your profile, and you
approve it, because a profile is its owner's to approve. Keep each half to about one page.

## Taste (applies when directing)

1. **In five words:** three to five adjectives for the work you want.
2. **Love and hate, calibrated:** pairs with an example each ("too plain" means..., "too busy" means...).
3. **References:** studios, films, your own past work, and what to take from each.
4. **Motion signature:** curves you reach for, entrances and exits, holds, energy, what "smooth" means to you.
5. **Look signature:** shading, depth, palette approach, type, grain, glow and texture tolerance.
6. **Overrides of core rules:** which core rules you set aside, and why.

## Working style (applies when running a session)

1. **Who:** role, main tools and versions, installed fonts and plug-ins.
2. **Autonomy map:** what to do without asking / plan first / needs an explicit go / never.
3. **Review cadence:** when to show work, in what form (stills, reduced-resolution video, A/B options), how notes arrive.
4. **Vocabulary:** your shorthand and what each phrase asks for.
5. **Known failure modes:** the corrections you find yourself repeating, one line each.
6. **Cost tolerance:** render time, rough resolution, file weight.
7. **Writing style:** for documents, handoffs and messages.
